Computational Cancer Biology and Epigenomics

We develop computational methods that read cell identity from the epigenome: which cells make up a tissue or a tumour, where a cancer came from, and how individual cells differ.

Read our latest post Publications

Research foci

01

Methylome deconvolution

Estimating the cell-type composition of DNA methylation data, from aggregated profiles down to individual sequencing reads, with and without reference panels.

02

Cancer cell-of-origin inference

Using the epigenetic memory carried by tumour cells to trace cancers back to the normal cell types they arose from.

03

Single-cell and spatial epigenomics

Quantifying epigenetic heterogeneity and integrating multiomic measurements in single cells and tissues.

04

Oncohistone malignancies

How mutations in histone genes, such as H3.3-G34W in giant cell tumours of bone, rewire the epigenetic landscape.
