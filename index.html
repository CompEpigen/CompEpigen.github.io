What we work on

Research focus areas

Methylome deconvolution

Methods to resolve cell-type and state composition from bulk methylation profiles.

Cancer cell-of-origin inference

Computational approaches to connect tumor epigenomes to normal developmental lineages.

Single-cell and spatial epigenomics

Analysis frameworks for multiomic profiling at cellular and tissue resolution.

Oncohistone malignancies

Epigenetic dysregulation and chromatin mechanisms in histone-driven cancers.
